\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:futurework}

Future work will integrate the authorization primitive with secure boot and firmware
update flows to ensure the trigger is placed in early boot and that updates cannot
bypass the check. We will also evaluate robustness against fault and glitch attacks by
adding attempt counters, rate limiting, and tamper-evident audit logs.

On the engineering side, we will quantify hardware cost and latency across target
microcontrollers and expand automated toolchain tests to validate encoding, placement,
and failure semantics across compiler versions. We also plan to extend the host policy
model to support per-firmware keys and role-based authorization for operators.
