\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:futurework}

Future work will formalize device-bound provisioning: reliable extraction of device
identity, key derivation, secure enrollment into protected non-volatile storage, and
recovery procedures that prevent unauthorized re-binding. The staged authorization
sequence shown in the diagrams can be implemented with strict ordering and timeouts to
strengthen resistance to partial replay.

We will also extend the host database into a policy service with richer audit metadata
(firmware hashes, toolchain version, and per-device authorization history). Additional
evaluation is needed to quantify area/power overhead and authorization latency, and to
add regression tests that verify encoding, placement, and failure behavior of the
authorization instruction.
